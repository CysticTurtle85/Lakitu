# 📝About

### Beat a Lakitu, take its cloud, rule the skies.
Lakitus now drift over the mountains on their clouds, throwing Spiny Eggs at anyone walking below. Defeat one and it might drop its **Lakitu Cloud**: a mount you can ride anywhere, and the higher you fly, the faster it goes.

## ☁️Features
- **Lakitu Cloud:** use it to summon your cloud and hop on. Fly the way you look with WASD, Space to rise, Shift to sink, and use it again to hop off (you drift down with Slow Falling).
- **Altitude speed:** normal speed at sea level, up to 3× near the build limit and half speed deep underground. Always 3× in the End and 0.5× in the Nether. Speed potions stack on top.
- **Rain clouds:** rain or water turns your cloud grey and slows it to half speed.
- **Spiny Eggs:** throw them from your cloud for 5 damage each. Lakitus drop 2-4 (more with Looting), or craft them.
- **Lakitus:** found high in the mountains, about as rare as a Ghast. They leave cloud riders alone unless you hit them first, and killing one gives a 10% chance (more with Looting) of its cloud.
- **Cloud health:** your cloud has its own health that slowly heals, whether you're riding it or it's stowed. If it pops, it re-forms 30 seconds later.
- 4 advancements, and every number can be changed in `config/lakitu.json`.
